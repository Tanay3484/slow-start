Field guide

Everything that blocks the path

Every day one wild creature stands between you and your step goal. Its health is the goal; every step you log is a hit. Catch it and it follows Slumbrock home. Where you walk decides who turns up.

Your giant

Slumbrock

His ability is called Slow Start: he takes a while to get going, and then he does not stop. Your XP wakes him through four stages, and his Speed and Power both start at half.
